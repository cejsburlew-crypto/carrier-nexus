ℹ️ When is a BOC-3 Update Required?

  • Your operating authority changes (new MC/DOT number, authority type changes)
  • You add operations in states beyond your current filing coverage
  • Your blanket agent withdraws or changes coverage
  • Your agent's contact information changes
  • Otherwise, the BOC-3 is a ONE-TIME filing — no annual renewal required
🔗 Resources & Reference
A BOC-3 designates a process agent in each state where you operate. Process agents accept legal documents on behalf of your company on service of process matters. A BOC-3 filing is required for interstate operating authority and must be on file with the FMCSA before your authority becomes active.
