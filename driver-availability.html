Privacy & Visibility
  • Your availability profile is visible to approved dispatchers and brokers only — never public.
  • Toggle "Available" OFF at any time to immediately hide yourself from the network.
  • Your contact information is never shared without your dispatcher's approval.
  • Equipment types and radius help dispatchers find the right driver for each load.
How to Get More Loads
  • Set your availability status to ON between loads so dispatchers can find you.
  • Specify accurate equipment types — dispatchers filter by equipment.
  • Set your work radius (Local/Regional/OTR) to get matched with nearby freight.
  • Keep your CDL and medical card up to date in My Documents.
  • A complete bio increases your chance of being selected for premium loads.
Network Rules
  • Only active Carrier Nexus drivers appear in the network.
  • Availability status auto-resets to "Not Available" after 7 days if not refreshed.
  • False availability claims may result in network removal.
  • Contact dispatchers through Nexus Connect — no direct external contact sharing.
