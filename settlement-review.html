🧠 Learning Feedback Loop
Every revision and approval is stored as structured feedback. This builds a training dataset that flags settlements likely to need changes before they reach Executive Leadership. The more reviews completed, the smarter the pre-screening becomes.

How it works: When Exec returns a settlement with notes, the system logs what was flagged. Over time it identifies patterns — e.g., "permit costs questioned repeatedly" or "specific routes have mileage discrepancies" — and surfaces those as warnings during Operations review.
