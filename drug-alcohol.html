FMCSA Drug & Alcohol Clearinghouse Requirements
Employers must conduct a full query before a CDL driver operates a CMV for the first time (pre-employment). Employers must conduct a limited query at least annually for all currently employed CDL drivers. Drivers must provide electronic consent via the Clearinghouse portal for full queries.

Access the Clearinghouse: https://clearinghouse.fmcsa.dot.gov
