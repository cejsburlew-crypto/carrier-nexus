📐 How This Calculator Works
Trailer Tandem Reaction = Load Weight × (CG distance from kingpin ÷ kingpin-to-tandem distance). The farther back the load CG, the more weight goes to the trailer tandems.
Kingpin Reaction = Load Weight − Trailer Tandem Reaction. This force is transferred through the fifth wheel to the tractor.
Steer Axle = Tractor steer unladen + Kingpin Reaction × (kingpin-to-drive ÷ tractor wheelbase). A rear-heavy load shifts weight toward drive axles and away from steers.
Drive Tandem = Tractor drive unladen + Kingpin Reaction × (steer-to-kingpin ÷ tractor wheelbase).
Trailer Tandem Total = Trailer empty tandem weight (60% of trailer GVW) + Load trailer reaction.
Legal limits (Federal Bridge Formula basis): Steer 12,000 lbs • Drive Tandem 34,000 lbs • Trailer Tandem 34,000 lbs • Gross 80,000 lbs.
Note: This is a simplified beam-model estimate. Real-world results depend on fifth-wheel position, suspension type, trailer balance, and cargo density distribution. Always verify with a certified scale.
