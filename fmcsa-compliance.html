FMCSA QC API Key — Required for Live Carrier Lookups
Free registration at ask.fmcsa.dot.gov — response within 1 business day
The FMCSA Query Center (QC) API returns real-time carrier data in JSON format. It's free to register. Once configured, Carrier Nexus can look up any carrier's safety record, authority status, and insurance directly — no browser redirects needed.
How to Get Your API Key
  1. Go to the FMCSA registration page: ask.fmcsa.dot.gov — Register for QC API — opens in new tab
  2. Complete the short registration form with your company name, USDOT#, and email address
  3. FMCSA will email your webKey — usually within 1 business day
  4. Paste it in the field below and click Save Configuration
