Ten operational improvements from day one.

01
Faster Billing Closeout
Billing packets auto-assemble when all required documents are uploaded. One click sends to broker, billing, and accounting.
02
Tire Failure Intelligence
Track every tire by wheel position, odometer, and load. Identify failure patterns before they cost you a breakdown.
03
Full Operational Visibility
Fleet Command shows every active load, missing POD, billing status, and maintenance alert in one dashboard.
04
POD Collection in <60s
Driver Command is built for under 60 seconds. Drivers tap, photograph, confirm. POD lands in Drive and links to the load automatically.

Broker + Customer History
Every load, rate, complaint, contact, and communication permanently connected to the broker profile in your CRM.

Fuel Cost Per Mile
Tank, WEX, EFS, and Comdata integrated. See cost per mile by truck, driver, and route. Flag anomalies automatically.
07
Reduced Downtime
PM reminders based on actual ELD mileage. Fault codes connected to repair history. Maintenance patterns become visible early.
08
Searchable Operational Memory
Every event, document, contact, conversation, and cost permanently linked and searchable. The knowledge stays when people leave.

WhatsApp as Operational Record
Field communication stays in WhatsApp. Carrier Nexus captures it, links it to loads, drivers, and repairs. Nothing disappears.
