Multi-Tenant Data Isolation

Each company in Carrier Nexus has its own isolated data partition. Documents, loads, settlements, expenses, and driver records are scoped to the active company. Switching companies changes which data set is active across all pages. No cross-company data leakage occurs — each company's data is stored under a separate localStorage namespace and Drive vault root.
